# Kavach 🛡️ Scam check for students

![domain:security](https://img.shields.io/badge/security-3D8BD3) ![tech:multi-agent](https://img.shields.io/badge/multi--agent-3D8BD3)

Paste any suspicious **internship/job offer, UPI payment request, SMS, email or "digital arrest" call script**. Kavach tells you whether it is a scam, shows the evidence, and gives you a ready-to-file cybercrime complaint.

**How it works:** Kavach fans your message out to two specialist agents:
- **link-forensics**: domain age (RDAP), brand lookalike domains, shorteners, risky TLDs, UPI IDs, free-mail recruiters
- **pattern-analyst**: Indian scam playbooks (fee-before-joining, task scams, CBI/customs impersonation, OTP/AnyDesk asks) plus ASI:One reasoning

**Example prompts**
- "I got this offer: Selected for Amazon WFH internship, pay ₹1,999 refundable deposit to hr.amazon@ybl within 24 hours"
- "Someone from CBI says a FedEx parcel in my name has drugs and I'm under digital arrest"

**Output:** risk score, evidence list, what to do now (1930 helpline, cybercrime.gov.in, Sanchar Saathi Chakshu) and a pre-filled complaint.
